WordSpun Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026 · Developer: Unpossible Apps

WordSpun is a relaxing offline word puzzle game: crossword levels you fill from a letter wheel, and a daily word search. It has no account, no cloud service and no servers of its own. This policy explains what the app does and doesn't do with information.

Your data stays on your device

Your game progress (levels started and solved, words found, daily puzzles and solving times), your coins, energy and streak, and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Puzzles are generated on the device from a built-in word list, so playing never needs the internet. We don't operate any servers for WordSpun, so we can't see, access, back up or recover your progress, and we never receive it.

Information processed by Google services in the app

  • Google Play Billing processes purchases of WordSpun Pro subscriptions (monthly or yearly). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never receive your payment details. The app checks with Google Play on your device to confirm whether your subscription is active.
  • Advertising. Version 1.0 shows no ads, and the advertising SDK included in the app is never started. A future version may show ads to free players through Google AdMob. If it does, this policy will be updated before that version is released, you'll be asked for consent where the law requires it, and WordSpun Pro will remain ad-free.

Apart from Google Play Billing, the app makes no network connections.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional) are used only for the daily puzzle reminder, if you switch it on in Settings. The app asks for this permission once, when you turn the reminder on.
  • Network access is used only by Google Play Billing (and, if ever enabled, the advertising SDK described above).
  • WordSpun doesn't access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or other files. A backup file is written only where you choose, and a backup is read only when you pick it.

Home-screen widgets

If you add a WordSpun widget, it shows your streak, level progress, coins and energy on your home screen. The widget reads this from the app's storage on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

Things we don't do

  • No account or sign-in, and no analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
  • No leaderboards or social features, so your play is never shared with other players.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal information, and we don't build a profile of you.

Backups

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for WordSpun. To move to a new phone, use Settings > Back up progress to save a backup file wherever you choose, such as a folder on your device or a cloud drive you pick. That destination is governed by its own privacy policy. Backup files contain your game progress and settings and are not encrypted. We never receive these files. Your Pro subscription is not stored in backups; it's restored from Google Play.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app permanently removes all of its data from your device. To end a WordSpun Pro subscription, cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

Children

WordSpun is a general-audience game that collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
